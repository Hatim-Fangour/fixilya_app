## Executive Summary

The on-demand home services marketplace sector reached **$5.29B in 2024**, projected to hit **$9.03B by 2028** (14.3% CAGR). The category is converging on four axes: **AI-powered matching, super-app bundling, trust/verification as a competitive moat, and subscription-based retention**.

**Fixilya's competitive position:** There is **no established local competitor in Morocco's handyman marketplace space**. The primary competition is informal (WhatsApp-based discovery, word of mouth). This is a significant first-mover advantage — but it requires Fixilya to educate the market, not just compete for existing digital demand.

**Overall Fixilya completion:** ~70% of a competitive MVP. Core booking + communication + profiles are solid. The critical gaps are: live payment processing, real-time GPS tracking, fixed/transparent pricing, and recurring bookings. These four features block monetization and long-term retention.

## Competitor Profiles

### 1. TaskRabbit (US — Market Leader)

**Scale:** 200,000+ service providers, 6 countries, ~$75M revenue (2025)
**Owner:** IKEA Group

**Business Model:**
- Taskers set their own hourly rates ($18–$75/hr)
- Clients pay 15% service fee + 7.5% trust fee on each invoice
- Taskers keep 100% — platform charges client-side only
- Algorithmic surge pricing for same-day bookings

**Key Features:**
- Client chooses the specific provider (not auto-assigned)
- Mandatory background checks via Checkr (SSN + 7-year criminal lookback)
- Elite Tasker badge — top 15% by metro category (based on accept/performance score)
- In-app chat with photo/video sharing
- Pricing Guidance Algorithm — suggests competitive rates to new Taskers
- IKEA flat-rate furniture assembly (exclusive captive demand channel)
- Same-day premium booking option

**Relevant Learnings for Fixilya:**
- Provider-set pricing (not fixed) works when the market is mature and clients can compare profiles
- Background check as a hard trust signal — removes the #1 adoption blocker
- Service-fee model (charge client, not provider) builds supply faster
- IKEA-style partnerships for guaranteed volume — Fixilya could target Moroccan furniture chains (KITEA, AZURA)

---

### 2. Urban Company / UrbanClap (India, UAE, Saudi Arabia)

**Scale:** 50,000+ trained professionals, ₹1,260 crore (~$150M) revenue FY25, 36% YoY growth
**Most Relevant:** Closest structural model for Morocco/MENA

**Business Model:**
- Fixed/flat prices per service (e.g., "Home Deep Clean 2BR = 450 AED") — the client never sees hourly rates
- 20–30% commission per booking from professional
- B2B supply chain: professionals buy UC-approved tools/consumables through the app (₹188 crore revenue FY25)
- UC Plus Membership: annual client subscription for discounts + priority booking (₹98 crore revenue FY25)

**Key Features:**
- Fixed-price model: zero pricing ambiguity; client sees final price before confirming
- Platform assigns the professional (clients don't choose) — UC controls quality, not individual reputation
- Police verification of all service partners (not just database check)
- UC Academy: 247 training classrooms, professionalizes the workforce
- InstaHelp (2025): 10–15 min dispatch for domestic help, 50,000+ daily bookings
- Branded uniforms + UC-supplied tools — visible trust signal at doorstep
- UC's own product brands (Native, Elysian, Crave): cleaning products sold to professionals

**Relevant Learnings for Fixilya:**
- **Fixed-price model is the right model for Morocco** — Moroccan consumers distrust variable pricing; a fixed "Plumber call = 250 MAD" removes negotiation and friction
- Training + verification creates a quality guarantee the market will pay for
- B2B supply chain (sell tools/supplies to handymen) is a future revenue stream
- Subscription tier (UC Plus equivalent) is the retention mechanism that drives 75% repeat revenue
- Auto-assignment vs. client choice: for early-market Moroccan adoption, auto-assignment reduces decision fatigue

### 3. Thumbtack (US)

**Scale:** 300,000+ professionals, $400M revenue FY2024, 500+ categories

**Business Model:**
- Pay-per-lead credits for professionals
- Promoted listing fees for boosted visibility
- Service fee on in-platform payments

**Key Features:**
- AI-powered smart matching — auto-sends top 3 pros immediately based on skills, availability, history, price preferences
- AI Project Cost Estimator — gives ballpark price range before client contacts a pro
- Home Management Dashboard — tracks home details, maintenance history, scheduled work, personalized maintenance reminders
- **OpenAI Operator integration (Jan 2025)** — first home services company bookable via ChatGPT Agent interface
- **Amazon Alexa AI integration (Feb 2025)** — end-to-end voice booking
- Thumbtack Guarantee: up to $1M property damage protection + $100K pro liability insurance

**Relevant Learnings for Fixilya:**
- Home management dashboard ("my home profile") creates permanent retention — app becomes a utility, not just a booking tool
- AI price estimation is now expected before the first interaction
- Property damage guarantee removes consumer risk perception — even if small, the guarantee matters more than its monetary value
- Long-term: LLM integration (WhatsApp bot booking in Moroccan context > Alexa)

- 30+ service categories including healthcare at home (doctor on call, lab tests, IV infusion), beauty at home, car wash at home, pet grooming

- Real-time professional en-route tracking
- Arabic/English dual-language UI built for Gulf culture

**Relevant Learnings for Fixilya:**
- Healthcare at home (doctor on call) is a massive adjacent category in MENA — Morocco has real demand for this
- 75% repeat revenue is the target metric — recurring + subscription is how you get there
- "Super-app" positioning (home + beauty + health) is the endgame; start with home services
- Real-time tracking is a hard expectation set by Careem/Bolt/Glovo — not a feature, a requirement
- Profitability path: focus on categories with high repeat frequency (cleaning, laundry, basic maintenance)

## Morocco/MENA Market Context

### Payment Landscape

| Method | Morocco Adoption | Fixilya Support |
|---|---|---|
| Cash on delivery | Very high (~60% of online transactions) | ❌ (should add "pay at service" option) |
| Bank card (Visa/Mastercard debit) | Growing rapidly | ⚠️ Stripe framework only |
| CMI (Centre Monétique Interbancaire) | Standard for Moroccan merchants | ❌ (CMI is preferred over Stripe locally) |
| Mobile wallet (Joro Cash, Inwi Money, mTierce) | Growing | ❌ |
| BNPL | Emerging (DONE.ma planning it) | ❌ |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | Limited | ❌ |

**Critical insight:** Stripe works in Morocco (via international card processing) but CMI is the preferred local acquirer for Moroccan bank cards. A pure Stripe-only integration will face higher failure rates for local debit cards.

### Cultural & UX Considerations

- **Darija (Moroccan Arabic dialect):** None of the global apps support it. Even adding basic Darija strings in the Arabic locale would create a meaningful differentiation.
- **Prayer time awareness:** Service scheduling UI should respect Adhan times — a feature no competitor has built for Morocco.
- **Gender preference for service providers:** A significant UX consideration for female clients. Female-preferred professional option (like Justlife offers in UAE) would drive adoption among a key demographic.
- **Cash-first trust:** First-time users prefer cash — allow "pay at service" to lower the trust barrier, then upsell to in-app payment over time.
- **Ramadan scheduling:** Booking patterns change dramatically during Ramadan; proactive handling (adjusted hours, shorter slots) signals cultural awareness.

## Recommended Features to Add

Features are organized into three tiers: **Critical** (blocks monetization or retention), **High-Value** (clear ROI, differentiator in local market), and **Strategic** (long-term moat).

---

### TIER 1 — CRITICAL (Must Ship First)

#### 1.1 Live Payment Processing

**The gap:** Payment service framework exists (Stripe intent creation, webhook) but is not connected end-to-end. No live payment flow in the app.

**What to build:**
- Complete the Stripe integration: payment sheet (client-side SDK) → payment intent confirmed → receipt stored in Firestore
- Add "pay at service (cash)" as a parallel option — critical for Moroccan market adoption
- Store payment records (amount, status, method, bookingId) in a `payments` Firestore collection
- Show digital receipt in booking details after completion
- Add CMI (Centre Monétique Interbancaire) as secondary gateway for local Moroccan bank cards

**Architecture additions:**
- Frontend: Flutter `stripe_payment` or `flutter_stripe` SDK, PaymentSheet widget
- Backend: Complete `payment-service` webhook handling + receipt generation
- New Firestore collection: `payments` (bookingId, amount, currency, method, stripeIntentId, status, createdAt)

---

#### 1.2 Fixed/Upfront Pricing Per Service Category

**The gap:** Handymen currently set hourly rates. Clients see a rate but not a total cost upfront. This causes friction and distrust — the #1 complaint on every handyman app globally.

**What to build:**
- Service category catalog with predefined service items (e.g., "Unclog kitchen sink drain — 150 MAD", "Install ceiling fan — 200 MAD")
- Handyman sets their own rates per service item, or uses platform suggested rates
- Booking creation flow shows: selected service → estimated price → confirmation (no surprises)
- Allow price range for complex jobs ("Bathroom tile install — 800–1,500 MAD depending on area")

**Architecture additions:**
- New Firestore collection: `service_categories` (id, name_ar, name_fr, name_en, icon, basePrice, description)
- New Firestore collection: `handyman_services` (handymanId, categoryId, customPrice, isOffered)
- New user-service routes: `GET /users/handyman/:id/services`, `PUT /users/handyman/services`
- Frontend: service picker in booking flow, price preview widget

---

#### 1.3 Real-Time GPS Tracking (Professional En Route)

**The gap:** Fixilya has GPS for discovery (finding nearby handymen) but no live tracking after booking is accepted. This is a hard expectation set by Careem/Bolt/Glovo in the MENA market.

**What to build:**
- After booking is accepted: handyman's app shares live GPS coordinates
- Client's app shows map with handyman moving toward the address in real-time
- ETA calculation based on distance + traffic
- "Handyman is X minutes away" push notification at key distance thresholds (10 min, 5 min, arrived)
- Auto-stop sharing when booking is marked as started

**Architecture additions:**
- New Firestore document path: `bookings/{bookingId}/location` (lat, lon, updatedAt, eta) — updated every 10s by handyman app
- Frontend: Firestore StreamBuilder listening to location doc → updates map marker
- Backend: No server change needed (Firestore direct write from handyman app) — optionally add `POST /bookings/:id/location` for server-side validation
- Privacy: location sharing strictly bounded to accepted booking duration

---

#### 1.4 Recurring Bookings / Subscriptions

**The gap:** All bookings are one-time. The highest-retention feature in every competitor is recurring scheduling.

**What to build:**
- Recurring booking option at booking creation: one-time / weekly / bi-weekly / monthly
- If recurring: auto-create next booking on completion of current one, with same handyman and same details
- Client can pause/cancel the recurring series from bookings history
- "My regular handyman" label in client profile for the most-booked provider

**Architecture additions:**
- Booking model extension: `isRecurring` (bool), `recurrenceInterval` (weekly/biweekly/monthly), `seriesId` (UUID linking all bookings in a series), `nextBookingDate`
- Booking-service: CRON job (or Cloud Functions) to auto-create next booking in series on completion
- New route: `PUT /bookings/:id/pause-series`, `DELETE /bookings/:id/cancel-series`
- Frontend: recurring toggle in booking screen, series management in booking history

---

#### 1.5 Dispute & Refund System

**The gap:** No dispute or complaint mechanism exists. If a job goes wrong, there is no path for the client. This is a trust-killer and a liability.

**What to build:**
- After booking completion: "Report an issue" button in booking details (visible for 48h after completion)
- Issue categories: "Work not completed", "Quality problem", "Professional didn't show", "Safety concern", "Other"
- Photo/video upload as evidence
- Admin review queue — flagged bookings appear in admin dashboard for resolution
- Outcomes: full refund, partial refund, free rebooking with different handyman
- Escalation path: auto-escalate if unresolved after 72h

**Architecture additions:**
- New Firestore collection: `disputes` (bookingId, clientUid, handymanUid, category, description, photos[], status, resolution, createdAt)
- New booking-service routes: `POST /bookings/:id/dispute`, `GET /bookings/:id/dispute`
- New admin routes: `GET /admin/disputes`, `PUT /admin/disputes/:id/resolve`
- Frontend: dispute form in booking details, admin dispute management page

---

### TIER 2 — HIGH VALUE (Competitive Differentiators)

#### 2.1 Handyman Verification Badges

**The gap:** Handymen have profiles but no trust signals beyond reviews (which are sparse on a new platform). Background checks and skill certifications are the #1 trust mechanism.

**What to build:**
- **ID Verified badge:** Admin confirms national ID scan matches profile
- **Skills Certified badge:** Admin manually certifies claimed skills after onboarding review
- **Top Rated badge:** Auto-awarded at 4.8+ average rating with 10+ reviews
- **Newcomer badge:** First 90 days on platform, encourages clients to give first-timers a chance
- **Subscription Pro badge:** Handymen who upgrade to a paid plan

**Architecture:**
- Add `verificationStatus` and `badges[]` to `handymen` Firestore document
- New admin route: `PUT /admin/handymen/:id/verify`
- Frontend: badge icons on profile cards and profile pages

---

#### 2.2 Before/After Work Photos

**The gap:** No photo capture flow during a booking. This is both a quality assurance tool and a marketing asset (handyman builds portfolio automatically).

**What to build:**
- When booking status changes to "in-progress": handyman sees "Add before photos" prompt
- When booking is marked "complete": handyman sees "Add completion photos" prompt
- Photos are stored in Cloudinary under `bookings/{bookingId}/before` and `bookings/{bookingId}/after`
- Photos are visible to both client (in booking details) and on handyman's public profile portfolio
- Optional: client can add their own "issue photos" at booking creation

**Architecture:**
- Extend `bookings` Firestore document: `beforePhotos[]`, `afterPhotos[]`
- New media-service endpoint: `POST /media/booking/:id/photos` (type: before|after)
- Frontend: camera integration at status-change points in booking flow

---

#### 2.3 Client "Fixilya Plus" Subscription

**Based on:** Handy Select ($10/mo → 20% off), Urban Company Plus (priority booking + discounts)

**What to build:**
- Monthly subscription: ~49 MAD/month (or 399 MAD/year)
- Benefits: 10–15% discount on all bookings, priority matching (faster response), exclusive handyman profiles
- Managed through Stripe Subscription API (or CMI recurring)
- Badge on client profile indicating subscriber status
- Handyman sees "Plus client" label — incentivizes extra care

**Architecture:**
- New Firestore collection: `subscriptions` (clientUid, plan, status, stripePriceId, stripeSubscriptionId, currentPeriodEnd)
- New payment-service routes: `POST /payments/subscribe`, `DELETE /payments/unsubscribe`, `GET /payments/subscription`
- Frontend: subscription management in client profile settings

---

#### 2.4 Smart Handyman Matching Algorithm

**The gap:** Clients browse a list. No intelligence about which handyman is the best fit for their specific request.

**What to build:**
- Matching score computed per handyman for each booking request based on:
  - Distance to client address (closer = higher score)
  - Skill match: does the handyman offer the requested service category?
  - Availability: is the handyman available at requested time slot?
  - Response rate: historically how often does this handyman accept bookings?
  - Rating: weighted average of last 20 reviews
  - Recency: active on platform in last 7 days?
- Default sort on handyman list = matching score (not alphabetical or recent)
- "Recommended" tag on top-matched handymen

**Architecture:**
- Matching score computed in user-service `GET /users/handyman/all` or a new `GET /users/handyman/recommended` endpoint
- Score formula: `(1/distance)*0.3 + skillMatch*0.25 + availability*0.2 + responseRate*0.15 + rating*0.1`
- No ML framework needed at this scale — pure algorithm with Firestore data

---

#### 2.5 WhatsApp Bot Booking

**The gap:** Morocco has ~90% WhatsApp penetration. Building a booking channel on WhatsApp captures users who will never install the app.

**What to build:**
- WhatsApp Business API (Meta Cloud API) — webhook for incoming messages
- Claude API (or Gemini) for intent parsing: extract service type, city, date/time from natural language
- Bot flow: welcome → detect language (AR/FR/EN) → collect service details → show available handymen → confirm booking → send booking ID + Fixilya deep link
- Hand off to human support agent if bot can't resolve

**Architecture:**
- New microservice: `whatsapp-service` (Node.js)
- Meta WhatsApp Business API webhook
- Claude API for intent classification and entity extraction
- Creates a booking via internal booking-service API (service-to-service call)
- Sends confirmation message with deep link to app

---

#### 2.6 In-App Tip After Service

**Based on:** Handy (US), Uber Eats

**What to build:**
- After marking a booking complete: client sees "How was [Handyman Name]?" → rating → optional tip screen
- Tip amounts: 10 MAD / 20 MAD / 50 MAD / Custom
- Tip stored separately from booking payment, paid directly to handyman minus platform processing fee
- Handyman sees tip notification immediately

**Architecture:**
- Extend `payments` collection with `tipAmount`, `tipPaidToHandyman`
- New route: `POST /payments/tip` (bookingId, amount)
- Frontend: tip prompt screen after review submission

---

#### 2.7 Service Area Management for Handymen

**The gap:** Handymen appear in "nearby" search based on their stored city/location. There's no way for a handyman to define "I serve Casablanca + Mohammedia but not Rabat."

**What to build:**
- Handyman settings: define service area as list of cities or as a radius (e.g., 25km from my location)
- Booking requests from outside the service area are not sent to this handyman
- Client side: shows "Available in your area" vs. "Serves different zone" on handyman cards

**Architecture:**
- Add `serviceAreas[]` (city names) and `serviceRadius` (km) to `handymen` Firestore document
- Update `GET /users/handyman/nearby` to filter by serviceAreas when a radius filter is insufficient
- Frontend: service area picker in handyman settings (map-based or city multiselect)

---

#### 2.8 SMS Notifications (Critical for Morocco)

**The gap:** Only FCM push notifications. In Morocco, many users have low-end devices with limited background processing, killing push notification delivery. SMS guarantees delivery.

**What to build:**
- Triggered SMS for critical events: booking confirmed, handyman accepted/declined, handyman en route, booking completed
- SMS via Twilio API or a Morocco-local SMS gateway (e.g., OXP, OredooB2B) — local gateways are cheaper and have better delivery rates for Moroccan numbers
- SMS template in French + Arabic (Moroccan phone numbers are +212)
- Opt-in/opt-out in settings

**Architecture:**
- New `sms-service` microservice (or add to notification-service)
- Twilio/OXP API integration
- Notification-service emits events; sms-service subscribes via internal event bus or is called directly
- Add `smsNotifications` preference to user settings (already has `smsNotifications` boolean in handyman settings)

---

#### 2.9 Video Call Feature

**The gap:** Fixilya has in-app VoIP (a rare differentiator). Extending to video would allow remote diagnostics — "show me the problem before I come" — saving both parties time for complex jobs like plumbing or electrical.

**What to build:**
- Video call option alongside existing voice call
- Pre-booking video consultation: client can request a 10-min video call with a handyman to describe the problem before committing to a booking
- Agora RTC already supports video — adding video is largely UI work on the existing Agora infrastructure

**Architecture:**
- call-service: add `videoEnabled` flag to `POST /calls/initiate` and Agora token options
- call-screen.dart: add camera toggle, video tile widgets (Agora provides Flutter widgets)
- New optional pre-booking flow: "Request a video consultation before booking"

---

#### 2.10 Home Profile (Client-Side)

**Based on:** Thumbtack's home management dashboard (their primary retention mechanism)

**What to build:**
- Client can create a "home profile": type (apartment/house/villa), size (m²), floor, district, number of rooms/bathrooms
- Auto-attached to every booking (handyman knows what to expect)
- Booking history attached to property → "last plumber who worked here was X"
- Maintenance checklist: list of recurring tasks (change AC filters every 3 months, etc.) with due date reminders
- "My Trusted Handymen" list: clients can flag a handyman as trusted → appears at top of search results for that client

**Architecture:**
- New Firestore collection: `client_homes` (clientUid, type, size, district, rooms, bathrooms, maintenanceItems[])
- New user-service routes: `GET /users/client/home`, `POST /users/client/home`, `PUT /users/client/home`
- Frontend: home setup wizard in client profile, maintenance reminder notifications
